# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Patent attorneys, corporate IP teams, and investors/founders tracking invention activity. Their context: scanning large volumes of patent data to find signals — filing trends, company R&D moves, expiring opportunities, and notable inventions. They need confidence-labeled estimates, not speculation.

## Product Purpose

Invention Index 8 turns global patent activity into readable market intelligence. It surfaces filing trends, company moves, expiring opportunities, and notable inventions with explicit confidence labels, source citations, and honest uncertainty — before these signals become obvious.

## Brand Personality

- **Analytical** — precise, evidence-backed, no hand-waving
- **Restrained** — calm confidence, never loud, lets the data speak
- **Professional** — built for serious practitioners, not hype-driven

## Anti-references

- Consumer SaaS marketing sites with purple gradients, glow effects, and "AI-washing"
- Over-animated landing pages with scroll-hijacking and decorative motion
- Generic dark-mode "tech startup" aesthetic with neon accents
- Anything that feels like a template

## Reference aesthetic

Palantir — dark, data-forward, minimal color, typographic hierarchy, enterprise-caliber restraint. The interface should feel like a professional intelligence tool, not a marketing website.

## Design Principles

1. **Data first, decoration never** — the content is the visual interest; chrome stays minimal
2. **Honest uncertainty** — confidence is labeled, never hidden; low confidence is surfaced, not buried
3. **Typographic hierarchy over color** — structure comes from type scale and weight, not hue
4. **Restraint earns trust** — understated design signals competence to professional users
5. **Dark by default** — consistent dark mode throughout; no section-level theme inversions

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text)
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored throughout
- Keyboard navigable, visible focus indicators
- Semantic HTML structure
