## V2 Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Semantic Search (Phase 1)

DeepSeek embeddings on all patents. pgvector. Hybrid ranking: vector + keyword + recency + score. Foundation for chatbot, graph, wiki.

### Pillar 2 — Research Paper Intelligence (Phase 2-3)

Second major corpus alongside patents.

**Data sources:** OpenAlex (broad metadata), arXiv (preprints), Semantic Scholar (citations/recommendations), Crossref (DOI normalization). PubMed/PMC later.

**Schema:** `research_papers`, `paper_authors`, `paper_citations`, `paper_topics`, `paper_embeddings`, `paper_patent_links`

**Paper-to-patent matching:** Semantic similarity, shared terms, shared topics, citation links, author/inventor overlap, time-sequence signals.

### Pillar 3 — Patent Chatbot / Research Assistant (Phase 4)

SSE streaming. RAG over patents, papers, companies, wiki. Tool-calling layer for app navigation: `search_patents`, `open_patent`, `compare_companies`, `expand_graph`, `create_wiki_page`, `save_insight`. Cites every answer with links. Saves useful answers into wiki.

### Pillar 4 — Knowledge Graph (Phase 5)

D3.js. Progressive/query-driven (not thousands of nodes at once). Modes: company landscape, technology cluster, citation network, inventor network, expiring opportunity map, competitive whitespace. Chatbot can manipulate the graph.

### Pillar 5 — LLM Wiki (Phase 6)

Git-tracked markdown. Company pages, technology pages, research frontier pages, opportunity pages, weekly briefings. LLM builds and maintains. Obsidian-compatible.

### Pillar 6 — Community Intelligence Layer (Phase 7)

- Feature request board with upvoting (lifetime members: 1 submission + 1 vote per month)
- Public topic/company pages
- Shareable insight cards
- Watchlists with alerts
- Weekly digest emails
- Saved research boards

### Pillar 7 — Engagement & Polish (Phase 8)

- "Ask This Patent" contextual actions on every detail page
- Opportunity cards (Expiring Soon, Fast-Moving Company, Emerging Theme, etc.)
- Company intelligence pages with activity trends, top technologies, AI summaries
- Technology theme pages generated from clusters
- Onboarding flow
- SEO optimization
- Export/report generation
- Pricing hooks (Stripe integration)

## Feature Request System Spec

**Model:** `feature_requests` table

- `id`, `title`, `description`, `status` (open/in_progress/done/declined)
- `submitter_id` → users
- `upvote_count` (cached)
- `created_at`, `updated_at`

**Upvotes:** `feature_request_votes` table

- `request_id`, `user_id`, `created_at`
- Unique constraint on (request_id, user_id)

**Rules:**
- Lifetime members: 1 feature request submission per calendar month
- Lifetime members: 1 upvote per calendar month
- Free/Basic: can view and comment, cannot submit or upvote
- Comments: standard comments table on feature_requests

**API:**
- `GET /api/v1/feature-requests` — list with pagination, sort by votes/new
- `POST /api/v1/feature-requests` — create (lifetime only, rate-limited)
- `POST /api/v1/feature-requests/{id}/upvote` — upvote (lifetime only, rate-limited)
- `POST /api/v1/feature-requests/{id}/comments` — comment
- `GET /api/v1/feature-requests/{id}/comments` — list comments

**Frontend:** `/feature-requests` page with board view, sorted by votes. "Submit request" button (gated behind lifetime check). Comment thread per request.
