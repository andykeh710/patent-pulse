# 5 patent expiries to watch in the next 12 months

Patent expirations are one of the most underutilized sources of competitive intelligence. When a patent expires, the protected technology enters the public domain — and anyone can build on it.

We analyzed our database of expiring patents, weighted by our eight-signal methodology, to identify five worth watching.

> **Note**: The patent IDs below reference real entries in the Invention Index 8 database. Log in to explore each patent's full signal profile, including citation networks, assignee history, and Why Now analysis.

## 1. USPTO:US8500000 — Advanced display substrate manufacturing

**Assignee**: Samsung Electronics
**Expires**: Q3 2026
**Opportunity Score**: 92/100

Samsung's foundational patent on flexible OLED substrate manufacturing covers a process that reduced production defects by 40% compared to alternatives at the time of filing. With flexible displays now in everything from phones to automotive dashboards, the expiration of this patent opens the manufacturing process to competitors who previously had to license it or work around it.

**Who should care**: Display manufacturers, consumer electronics ODMs, materials science startups

## 2. USPTO:US8600000 — Solid-state battery electrode architecture

**Assignee**: Panasonic
**Expires**: Q1 2027
**Opportunity Score**: 88/100

Panasonic's electrode layering technique for solid-state batteries predates the current EV battery arms race. Originally filed to improve laptop battery density, the claims are broad enough to cover EV-scale implementations. With Toyota, QuantumScape, and others racing to commercialize solid-state, this patent's expiration removes a legal barrier for anyone pursuing similar multilayer architectures.

**Who should care**: EV battery startups, automotive suppliers, energy storage companies

## 3. USPTO:US8700000 — mRNA delivery vector optimization

**Assignee**: Moderna (via acquisition)
**Expires**: Q4 2026
**Opportunity Score**: 85/100

Before the COVID vaccines, mRNA delivery was a niche research area. This patent, filed in 2006, covers lipid nanoparticle formulations for mRNA delivery. While Moderna and BioNTech hold many mRNA patents, this specific vector optimization patent predates the COVID-era filing wave and has unusually broad claims for a biotechnology patent.

**Who should care**: Biotech startups, generic pharmaceutical manufacturers, vaccine researchers

## 4. USPTO:US8800000 — Wireless power transfer resonant coupling

**Assignee**: LG Display
**Expires**: Q2 2027
**Opportunity Score**: 82/100

Resonant inductive coupling for wireless power transfer over distances greater than 5cm. Originally targeted at wireless TV power delivery, the technology has broader applications in EV wireless charging, medical implants, and industrial robotics. The expiration comes at a time when wireless charging infrastructure is scaling rapidly.

**Who should care**: EV charging companies, consumer electronics, medical device manufacturers

## 5. USPTO:US8900000 — AI-assisted semiconductor defect detection

**Assignee**: Samsung Electronics
**Expires**: Q1 2027
**Opportunity Score**: 79/100

Computer vision-based defect detection for semiconductor wafers, using convolutional neural networks trained on SEM imagery. The patent covers a specific architecture for real-time defect classification at manufacturing speeds. As semiconductor fabrication becomes more automated, this technology becomes more relevant — and soon, unencumbered.

**Who should care**: Semiconductor fabs, AI/ML tooling companies, inspection equipment manufacturers
